## 4. Directions & Reaching the Campus
- **From Charbagh Railway Station (~28 km):** Head north via Ring Road / Mohibullapur towards Sitapur Road (NH-24). Frequent city buses and auto-rickshaws connect to BKT.
- **From Chaudhary Charan Singh Airport (~38 km):** Accessible via Shaheed Path to Sitapur Highway.
- **From Sitapur City (~60 km):** Travel south straight along NH-24 directly past the Bakshi ka Talab toll plaza.
