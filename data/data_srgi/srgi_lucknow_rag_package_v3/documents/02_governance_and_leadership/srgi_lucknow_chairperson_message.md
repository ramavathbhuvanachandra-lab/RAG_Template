# Message from the Chairperson — Mrs. Nirmala Singh Chauhan

## Scope
Official message, educational values, student engagement philosophy, and campus culture overview from Chairperson Mrs. Nirmala Singh Chauhan.

## 1. Chairperson Profile
- **Name:** Mrs. Nirmala Singh Chauhan
- **Designation:** Chairperson, S. R. Group of Institutions, Lucknow

## 2. Official Address to Prospective & Enrolled Students
> *"Today a reader, tomorrow a leader."*

*"At SRGI, we open minds to 'connect the dots', see the big picture and make new sense of life. We create a campus environment where students are:*
- *Exposed to exciting campus events that build social skills.*
- *Challenged by industry projects that open their eyes to real-world complexities.*
- *Stimulated with personal freedom to build confidence and add value to life.*

*I strongly encourage all prospective students to visit our campus, meet the Heads of Departments (HODs), Training & Placement team, Admission team, and student section officials to learn more about the academic programmes and rich opportunities waiting for them."*

## 3. Strategic Campus Initiatives
- **Holistic Development:** Creating spaces where cultural festivals, technical hackathons, and literary competitions complement daily academic curricula.
- **Student Mentorship:** Open-door policies across academic and administrative departments to assist students with curricular guidance and personal growth.
- **Women Empowerment:** Fostering a secure, inclusive, and encouraging environment for female technocrats and management scholars through dedicated residential and recreational facilities.
