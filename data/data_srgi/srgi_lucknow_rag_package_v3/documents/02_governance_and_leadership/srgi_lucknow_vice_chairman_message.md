# Message from the Vice-Chairman — Mr. Piyush Singh Chauhan

## Scope
Official address, global youth perspective, technological innovation mandate, and entrepreneurship vision of Vice-Chairman Mr. Piyush Singh Chauhan.

## 1. Vice-Chairman Profile
- **Name:** Mr. Piyush Singh Chauhan
- **Designation:** Vice-Chairman, S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow
- **Professional Identity:** Entrepreneur, Edupreneur, Youth Mentor
- **Public Speaking:** Renowned Speaker at TEDx and Josh Talks platforms

## 2. Official Message
> *"Wake up to a better tomorrow."*

*"I hope you will discover the exhilaration of the intellectual life and a chance to contribute to thought whose measure will move our descendants many years from now. I wish you all the best in your future endeavours.*

*We envision a time when youth from different regions are engaged in all sectors of technology and management where issues affecting young people are debated and decisions made. We create a network of youth empowered with skills and knowledge, to be able to contribute to a healthy economy and growth of society and nation.*

*We are moulding young minds to be active in global events where their opinions can be listened to and respected. We are committed to creating a vibrant and stimulating environment to live and learn."*

## 3. Youth Empowerment & Global Competencies
- **Startup Incubation:** Encouraging student-led entrepreneurial ventures and hackathons.
- **Skill Alignment:** Continuously restructuring technical labs to match emerging industry stacks (AI, cloud, machine learning, and automation).
- **Public Speaking & Thought Leadership:** Fostering communication excellence through student societies and guest lecture series.
