# SRGI Lucknow Board of Governors — Composition and Strategic Counsel

## Scope
Official membership, profiles, medical/academic credentials, and strategic guidance of the SRGI Board of Governors.

## 1. Governance Charter
The Board of Governors at SRGI Lucknow functions as the apex strategic policy body, steering institutional priorities, infrastructure expansion, quality benchmarks, and healthcare/technical integrations.

## 2. Distinguished Governors

### Dr. Vaibhav Pratap Singh
- **Qualifications:** MBBS, DNB (Radiodiagnosis), PDCC (Abdominal Imaging)
- **Designation:** Member, Board of Governors
- **Guiding Philosophy:** *"Today is your opportunity to build the tomorrow you want!"*
- **Counsel:** Emphasizes unearthing students' limitless potential through experiential learning, industry-specific curriculum alignment, and cultivating strong, ethical leaders prepared to address national and global challenges.

### Dr. Pallavi Singh
- **Qualifications:** MBBS, MS (Ophthalmology)
- **Designation:** Member, Board of Governors
- **Guiding Philosophy:** *"Purpose of education is to moralize and socialize the people."*
- **Counsel:** Underscores that technical and management education is essential for societal transformation, economic prosperity, and global progress. Champions the continuous development of world-class infrastructure and competitive laboratories to rival top international institutions.

## 3. Governance Responsibilities
1. Institutional accreditation, quality audit approvals, and statutory compliance.
2. Long-term capital investments in laboratory machinery, library automation, and campus expansion.
3. Healthcare educational synergies across Ayurvedic and medical units.
