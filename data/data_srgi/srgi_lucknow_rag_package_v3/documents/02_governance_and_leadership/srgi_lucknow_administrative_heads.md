# SRGI Lucknow Administrative Heads and Core Operational Team

## Scope
This document records the official administrative leadership and operational officers responsible for proctorial discipline, training and placement, academic records, finance, library resources, student transport, and residential hostels at S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow, derived directly from the official administrative directory.

## Executive Administrative Directorate
The day-to-day operations and student governance of SRGI Lucknow are steered by senior administrative officers stationed at the Main Administrative Block of the Sitapur Road campus.
