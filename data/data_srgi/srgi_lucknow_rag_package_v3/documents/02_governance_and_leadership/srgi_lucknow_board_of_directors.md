# SRGI Lucknow Board of Directors — Institutional Execution and Oversight

## Scope
This document details the members of the Board of Directors of S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow, their official designations, direct contact telephone lines, and verified executive messages, providing governance oversight across all academic, research, and operational units.

## Board of Directors Overview
The Board of Directors guides executive administration, academic standardization, faculty recruitment, student affairs, and corporate placement operations across all constituent units of SRGI Lucknow.

## Executive Governance Responsibilities
- **Continuous Quality Control:** Quality monitoring conducted through the Research and Resource Centre.
- **Industry Interface:** Establishing corporate Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) and technical partnerships.
- **Academic Monitoring:** Reviewing curriculum completion, sessional examination schedules, and faculty development.
