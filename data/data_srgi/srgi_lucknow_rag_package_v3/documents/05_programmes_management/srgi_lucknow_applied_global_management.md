# Applied Global Management Programme (AGMP)

## Scope
2-year advanced general management programme focused on international corporate strategy, contemporary corporate challenges, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Programme:** Applied Global Management Programme (AGMP)
- **Duration:** 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Focus:** Strategic management, cross-border corporate dynamics, global supply chains.

## 2. Curriculum Highlights
The AGMP is a rigorous management offering tailored for corporate readiness:
- Global Trade Policies, Cross-Cultural Management & International Finance.
- Data-driven Business Strategy & Corporate Governance.
- Comprehensive industry internship and live business consulting projects.
