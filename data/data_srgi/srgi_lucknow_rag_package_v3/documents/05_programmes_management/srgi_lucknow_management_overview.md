# Faculty of Management Studies — Academic Framework and Programmes

## Scope
Overview of management education at SRGI Lucknow, MBA, Integrated MBA (IMBA), Applied Global Management Programme (AGMP), and corporate leadership ecosystem.

## 1. Vision for Management Education
The Faculty of Management Studies at SRGI Lucknow (housed under SRIMT and SRM Business School) is committed to developing ethical, agile, and strategic business leaders capable of navigating complex domestic and multinational business environments.

## 2. Portfolio of Management Programmes

| Programme Name | Credential | Duration | Sanctioned Intake | Target Demographic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Master of Business Administration (MBA)** | Postgraduate Degree | 2 Years | 240 Seats | Graduates seeking corporate management careers |
| **Integrated MBA (IMBA)** | Dual Degree (BBA + MBA) | 5 Years | 30 Seats | School leavers (10+2) targeting leadership right after school |
| **Applied Global Management Programme (AGMP)** | Advanced Management Diploma | 2 Years | 60 Seats | Aspiring managers targeting global corporate operations |

## 3. Pedagogical Pillars
- Case study methodology derived from Harvard and top Indian management contexts.
- Corporate live internships, industry conclaves, and regular guest lectures by corporate CXOs.
- Soft-skill enhancement, personality development, psychometric profiling, and business simulation workshops.
