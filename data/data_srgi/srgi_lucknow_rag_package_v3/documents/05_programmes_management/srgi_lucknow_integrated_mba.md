# Integrated MBA (IMBA) — 5-Year Dual-Degree Management Programme

## Scope
5-year post-10+2 Integrated MBA programme combining undergraduate foundation and postgraduate management degrees, sanctioned intake of 30 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree Awarded:** Integrated Master of Business Administration (IMBA)
- **Duration:** 5 Academic Years (10 Semesters)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 30 Seats
- **Eligibility Entry:** 10+2 Passed from any recognized Board

## 2. Programme Distinctives
The Integrated MBA is designed specifically for students completing higher secondary school who wish to bypass separate undergraduate and postgraduate admission cycles:
- **Seamless Progression:** Early integration of managerial economics, accounting, and organisational psychology with advanced MBA coursework.
- **Cost & Time Efficiency:** Continuous mentorship over five years, preparing students for managerial roles by the age of 22–23.
- **Practical Immersions:** Multiple internships across the 5-year timeline.

## 3. Admission Criteria
Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Arts, Commerce, or Science) with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks.
