# Master of Business Administration (MBA) — 2-Year Full-Time Programme

## Scope
Full-time 2-year MBA programme, sanctioned intake of 240 seats, major specializations (Marketing, Finance, HR, IT, Operations), eligibility, and career outcomes.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- **Duration:** 2 Academic Years (4 Semesters)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 240 Seats
- **Affiliation:** Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
- **Approval:** AICTE, New Delhi

## 2. Programme Specializations
Students choose functional dual specializations in the second year:
- **Marketing Management:** Digital Marketing, Consumer Behaviour, Brand Strategy, Sales & Distribution.
- **Financial Management:** Corporate Finance, Investment Banking, Financial Derivatives, Security Analysis.
- **Human Resource Management (HRM):** Talent Acquisition, Industrial Relations, Organisational Development.
- **Information Technology:** Enterprise Systems, Business Analytics, E-Commerce Management.
- **Operations & Supply Chain:** Logistics Optimization, Quality Management, Project Management.

## 3. Eligibility & Admission Procedure
- **Academic Eligibility:** Recognized Bachelor's degree (any discipline) with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST).
- **Selection Basis:** Through CUET-PG / UPTAC counselling scores or institutional management quota merit.

## 4. Placement Opportunities
Graduates are hired across banking, financial services, FMCG, IT consultancy, retail, and edtech sectors.
