# Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) — 2-Year Entry-Level Pharmacy Programme

## Scope
D.Pharm course details, sanctioned intake of 50 seats, minimum qualification for Registered Pharmacist status in India, eligibility (10+2 PCB/PCM).

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Course Award:** Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)
- **Duration:** 2 Academic Years
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 50 Seats
- **Professional Recognition:** Minimum qualification required to practice as a Registered Pharmacist in India under the Pharmacy Act.

## 2. Curricular Scope
- **Year 1:** Pharmaceutics, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacognosy, Human Anatomy & Physiology, Social Pharmacy.
- **Year 2:** Pharmacology, Community Pharmacy & Management, Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology, Pharmacotherapeutics, Hospital & Clinical Pharmacy, Pharmacy Law & Ethics.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Candidates must have completed 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry along with Biology or Mathematics (PCB/PCM) from a recognized Board.
- Minimum age requirement of 17 years at the time of admission.
