# Nursing Programme — Clinical Healthcare & Ayurvedic Nursing

## Scope
Healthcare nursing training programme, 3.5-year duration, sanctioned intake of 100 seats, patient care protocols, hospital dispensary administration, and clinical skills.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Course:** Nursing / Ayurvedic Healthcare Nursing
- **Duration:** 3.5 Years (as published on official portal /medical/nursing)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 100 Seats
- **Objective:** Train competent healthcare assistants and professional nurses to serve in hospitals, dispensaries, and wellness centers.

## 2. Practical Training Modules
- Fundamentals of Nursing and Patient Care Protocols
- Clinical Anatomy, Hygiene, and Infection Control
- First Aid, Emergency Triage, and Hospital Ward Management
- Dispensing and Administration of Formulations
