# Faculty of Medical & Allied Health Sciences — Overview

## Scope
Overview of healthcare, Ayurvedic pharmacy, nursing, and homeopathy programmes operating under SRM Ayush Institute of Medical Sciences at SRGI Lucknow.

## 1. Departmental Mission
The Faculty of Medical & Allied Health Sciences (housed at SRM Ayush Institute of Medical Sciences) addresses the regional and national shortage of trained paramedical professionals, registered pharmacists, and skilled healthcare practitioners.

## 2. Academic Offerings Summary

| Course | Award Level | Duration | Sanctioned Intake | Professional Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)** | Diploma | 2 Years | 50 Seats | Registered Pharmacist under Pharmacy Council |
| **Nursing Programme** | Professional Healthcare | 3.5 Years (Diploma/Cert) | 100 Seats | Clinical Nurse / Ayurvedic Hospital Staff |
| **Diploma in Homeopathic Pharmacy (DHP)** | Healthcare Diploma | 2 Years | 100 Seats | Homeopathic Pharmacist & Manufacturing Specialist |

## 3. Clinical & Practical Training
Students gain practical exposure in institutional laboratories, medicinal herb gardens, and partner multi-speciality hospitals across Lucknow.
