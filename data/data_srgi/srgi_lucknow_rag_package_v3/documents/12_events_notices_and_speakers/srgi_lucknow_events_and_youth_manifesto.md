# Institutional Events and Uttar Pradesh Youth Manifesto

## Scope
Flagship collegiate events, Uttar Pradesh Youth Manifesto (December 2026), youth leadership conclaves, annual tech-fests and cultural exhibitions.

## 1. Uttar Pradesh Youth Manifesto (December 2026)
In December 2026, SRGI Lucknow hosts the historic **Uttar Pradesh Youth Manifesto**, convening youth leaders, educational innovators, industry heads, and policy makers from across the state.

### Thematic Pillars
- **Youth Representation:** Creating civic avenues for student voices in governance and educational policy.
- **Skill Alignment:** Closing the chasm between degree curricula and live technological requirements.
- **Innovation & Startups:** Providing seed grants and mentorship for youth-led ventures in Uttar Pradesh.

## 2. Annual Campus Festivals
- **Tech-Innovate:** Annual hackathons and technical project exhibitions.
- **Shaurya:** State-level multi-sport collegiate tournament.
- **Utsav:** Multi-day inter-collegiate cultural fest celebrating music, arts, and drama.
