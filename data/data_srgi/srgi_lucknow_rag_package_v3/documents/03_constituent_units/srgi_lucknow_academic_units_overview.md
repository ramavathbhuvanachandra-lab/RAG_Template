# SRGI Lucknow Constituent Academic Units and Institutional Network

## Scope
Comprehensive directory of official academic units, specialized schools, affiliated colleges, and external domains operating under the SR Group of Institutions ecosystem.

## 1. The Institutional Ecosystem
The S. R. Group of Institutions umbrella encompasses distinct colleges, professional schools, and foundational academies operating on the Lucknow campus and affiliated facilities:

| Academic Unit | Primary Focus & Degree Levels | Official Website / Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S. R. Institute of Management & Technology (SRIMT)** | Engineering (B.Tech, M.Tech), Management (MBA, IMBA), Vocational (B.Voc) | https://srimt.co.in/ |
| **SRM Business School (SRMBS)** | Postgraduate Business Management, Executive Studies & Leadership | https://srmbs.in/ |
| **SRM Ayush Institute of Medical Sciences (SRMAIMS)** | Ayurvedic Pharmacy, Nursing, Homeopathy, Allied Healthcare | https://srmaims.in/ |
| **SR Global School** | K-12 Quality Schooling, CBSE Board Curricula | https://www.srglobalschool.org/ |
| **SR International School & Sports Academy (SRISA)** | Sports Training, Residential Schooling & Athletic Coaching | https://www.srisa.in/ |

## 2. Academic Integration & Shared Campus Resources
All collegiate academic units share the core 65-acre Sitapur Road infrastructure:
- **Centralized Infrastructure:** Shared access to the Central Library (53,760+ volumes), Olympic-standard 6-track swimming pool, central auditoriums, sports arenas, and cafeteria.
- **Placement & Training Hub:** Unified recruitment pipeline through the Central Training & Placement Cell, ensuring students across SRIMT and SRMBS participate in unified placement drives.
- **Administrative ERP:** Centralized student management system powered by connectSR (https://connectsr.in/) for attendance, marks, fees, and sessional tracking.
