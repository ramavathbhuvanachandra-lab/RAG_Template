### Metric Context & Disambiguation
- **24,888+ Job Opportunities:** Represents the cumulative number of job openings, interview slots, and corporate selection opportunities generated across recruitment cycles (with an average of 2.3 offers per student).
- **95.6% Placement Rate:** Represents the verified proportion of eligible and registered graduating students who secured employment through on-campus and joint pool recruitment drives.
- **42.00 LPA Highest Package:** Top institutional offer secured by top-tier engineering talent.
- **4.00 LPA Average Package:** Mean compensation benchmark across multi-disciplinary recruitment batches.
