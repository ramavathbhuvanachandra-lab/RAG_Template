# Training & Placement Cell — Structure, Training Programmes and Corporate Relations

## Scope
Inception of T&P Cell in 2009, soft skills development, technical bootcamps, aptitude coaching, corporate internships, and interview readiness.

## 1. Cell Overview & Leadership
Established alongside the institution in 2009, the **Training & Placement Cell (T&P Cell)** at SRGI Lucknow bridges classroom academics and corporate hiring requirements. Led by Director (T&P) **Mr. Kaushal Chandra** (+91 9793000080), the cell operates continuously across the year.

## 2. Four-Pillar Training Architecture
1. **Technical Competency:** Intensive coding camps, full-stack workshops, AutoCAD/CAM training, and industry software certifications.
2. **Quantitative & Logical Aptitude:** Weekly problem-solving drills preparing students for corporate screening assessments (TCS NQT, AMCAT, eLitmus).
3. **Soft Skills & Communication:** Professional grooming, resume drafting, group discussions (GD), and mock personal interviews (PI) evaluated by industry veterans.
4. **Corporate Exposure:** Mandatory summer internships, live corporate industrial visits, and industry guest lectures.
