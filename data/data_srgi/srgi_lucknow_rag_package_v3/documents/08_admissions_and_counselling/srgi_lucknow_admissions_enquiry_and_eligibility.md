# SRGI Lucknow Admissions Procedure, Eligibility and Enquiry Channels

## Scope
Official admission procedure, direct application channels, online enquiry mechanism, official helpline numbers, and document verification requirements.

## 1. Official Admission Routes
Prospective students can secure admission through two primary channels:
1. **State Level Counselling (UPTAC / AKTU):** Based on JEE Main scores for B.Tech, CUET-PG for MBA, or respective state entrance examination rankings.
2. **Direct Institutional Merit Admission:** Eligible candidates may apply directly to SRGI Lucknow through the online portal or at the campus admission cell under the management / vacant seats quota as per regulatory norms.

## 2. Online Enquiry & Application Portal
- **Online Admission Form:** Available at https://srgi.in/enquiry

## 3. Mandatory Documents Required at Reporting
1. High School (10th) Marksheet & Passing Certificate
2. Intermediate (10+2) Marksheet & Passing Certificate
3. Graduation Marksheets & Degree (for MBA / M.Tech applicants)
4. Category / Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
5. Income Certificate (for scholarship & fee concession applicants)
6. Domicile Certificate (for U.P. state quota candidates)
7. Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
8. Character Certificate from the institution last attended
9. Aadhaar Card copy and recent passport-sized color photographs
