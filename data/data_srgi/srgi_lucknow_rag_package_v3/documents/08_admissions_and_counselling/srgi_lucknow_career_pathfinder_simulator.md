# SRGI Career Pathfinder and Course Selection Simulator

## Scope
Interactive career simulator tool provided on the official website helping prospective students map interests to engineering, management, or medical branches.

## 1. Tool Overview
The Career Pathfinder is an interactive web simulator integrated into the SRGI portal (https://srgi.in/careerpathfinder) designed to assist students and parents in identifying the optimal academic pathway based on personal aptitude, subject strengths, and career aspirations.

## 2. Functional Domains
- **Technology & Coding Aptitude:** Guides candidates towards B.Tech Computer Science (AI/ML), IT, and B.Voc Software Development.
- **Hardware & Systems:** Maps interest in circuits and robotics to ECE, Mechanical, or Electrical engineering.
- **Biological & Agricultural Sciences:** Directs candidates towards Biotechnology, Agricultural Engineering, or D.Pharm.
- **Commerce & Business Management:** Aligns candidates with Integrated MBA, B.Voc BFSI, or AGMP.
