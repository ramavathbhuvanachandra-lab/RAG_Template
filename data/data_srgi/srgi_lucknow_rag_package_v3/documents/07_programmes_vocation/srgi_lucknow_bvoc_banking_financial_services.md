# B.Voc in Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI)

## Scope
BFSI vocational degree, sanctioned intake of 120 seats, retail banking, insurance operations, stock markets, wealth management.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Vocation in Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance (BFSI)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 120 Seats
- **Duration:** 3 Years (6 Semesters)

## 2. Core Industry Competencies
- Retail & Commercial Banking Procedures
- Insurance Policy Underwriting & Claims Processing
- Financial Accounting, Tally ERP, and Taxation
- Mutual Funds, Equity Markets, and Wealth Management

## 3. Employment Partners
Graduates are recruited by private banks, micro-finance institutions, NBFCs, insurance brokers, and accounting firms.
