# Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc) Programmes — UGC Skill Education Framework

## Scope
UGC-approved 3-year B.Voc degree framework, 60% practical training and 40% theory ratio, work-integrated learning, multiple exit qualifications, and branches.

## 1. Programme Framework
- **Degree Awarded:** Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc)
- **Duration:** 3 Academic Years (6 Semesters)
- **Accreditation:** University Grants Commission (UGC) approved undergraduate degree
- **Instructional Ratio:** 60% Practical & Industry Training : 40% Academic Theory

## 2. Multiple Exit & Entry Points
Under the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF), students have flexible exit certifications:
- **1st Year Completion:** Diploma in Vocation
- **2nd Year Completion:** Advanced Diploma in Vocation
- **3rd Year Completion:** Full Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc) Degree

## 3. Branches Offered & Sanctioned Intakes

| B.Voc Discipline | Code | Sanctioned Intake | Primary Industry Sectors |
