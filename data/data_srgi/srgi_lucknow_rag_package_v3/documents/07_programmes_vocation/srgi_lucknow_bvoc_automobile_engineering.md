# B.Voc in Automobile Engineering

## Scope
Automobile Engineering vocational degree, sanctioned intake of 30 seats, vehicle diagnostics, modern automotive manufacturing, EV basics.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Vocation in Automobile Engineering
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 30 Seats
- **Duration:** 3 Years (6 Semesters)

## 2. Practical Focus
Hands-on automotive workshop training covering engine mechanics, transmission systems, automotive electrical circuits, electronic fuel injection (EFI), and vehicle service management.
