# Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) Programmes — Comprehensive Overview

## Scope
This document provides a comprehensive overview of the Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) undergraduate engineering programmes offered at S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow, including regulatory approvals, university affiliation, degree duration, branch-wise sanctioned seat intakes, documented source variances, and general eligibility criteria.

## Programme Framework
- **Degree Awarded:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)
- **Programme Duration:** 4 Academic Years (8 Semesters)
- **Regulatory Approval:** All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi
- **University Affiliation:** Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
- **Instructional Faculty:** S. R. Institute of Management & Technology (SRIMT)

## General Eligibility Criteria
- **10+2 Qualification:** Candidates must have passed the 10+2 examination from CBSE, ICSE, UP Board, or any recognized equivalent Board.
- **Mandatory Subjects:** Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, alongside one optional subject (Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or Technical Vocational subject).
- **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** At least 45% aggregate marks (40% for candidates belonging to SC/ST reserved categories).
- **Admission Modes:** Through Uttar Pradesh Technical Admission Counselling (UPTAC / JEE Main score) and Direct Institutional Merit Quota conforming to AKTU and state government norms.
