# B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE / EN)

## Scope
Electrical and Electronics Engineering details, power systems, smart grids, control engineering, power electronics, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EN / EEE)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Academic Focus
The department prepares engineers to design, operate, and innovate power generation systems, electric vehicles (EVs), renewable energy infrastructure, industrial automation, and micro-grid circuits.

## 3. Laboratory Suites & Practical Training
- Electrical Machines Laboratory (Transformers, DC/AC Motors, Synchronous Machines)
- Power Electronics & Electric Drives
- Control Systems Laboratory
- Power System Simulation & Protection
- Renewable Energy & Solar Photovoltaic Systems

## 4. Career Profiles
Power grid engineers, control systems engineers, EV powertrain designers, and public sector undertaking (PSU) technical officers.
