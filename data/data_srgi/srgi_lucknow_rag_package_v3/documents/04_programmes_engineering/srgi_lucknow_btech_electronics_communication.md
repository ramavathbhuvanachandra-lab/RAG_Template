# B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)

## Scope
ECE programme overview, semiconductor electronics, VLSI design, wireless communications, embedded IoT systems, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Curricular Scope
The ECE programme blends electronic hardware design with telecommunication networks, microprocessors, signal processing, and Internet of Things (IoT) applications.

## 3. Core Laboratory & Theory Areas
- Analog and Digital Communication Systems
- Microprocessors (8085/8086) and Microcontrollers (ARM / Arduino)
- Digital Signal Processing (DSP) & Image Processing
- VLSI Design & Verilog / VHDL Simulation
- Antenna Theory, Microwave Engineering & Optical Fiber Communications
- Embedded Systems & Robotics

## 4. Career Profiles
Graduates enter telecommunications corporations, semiconductor design units, consumer electronics manufacturers, and embedded software firms.
