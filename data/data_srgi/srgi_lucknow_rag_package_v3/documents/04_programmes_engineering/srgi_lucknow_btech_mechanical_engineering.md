# B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME)

## Scope
Mechanical Engineering programme structure, manufacturing technologies, CAD/CAM labs, thermal engineering, robotics, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Department Overview
Mechanical Engineering applies principles of physics, material science, and mathematics for the analysis, design, manufacturing, and maintenance of mechanical and thermal systems.

## 3. Practical Facilities & Workspaces
- Computer-Aided Design (CAD) & Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM) Laboratory
- Machine Shop (Lathes, Milling, Shaping, Drilling Machinery)
- Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machinery Lab
- Internal Combustion (IC) Engines and Heat Transfer Lab
- Material Testing & Metallurgy Lab

## 4. Career Opportunities
Graduates excel in automotive engineering, aerospace design, power plants, heavy machinery manufacturing, and HVAC engineering.
