# B.Tech in Biotechnology (BT)

## Scope
Biotechnology engineering curriculum, molecular biology, bioprocess engineering, bioinformatics, pharmaceuticals, sanctioned annual intake of 180 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Biotechnology (BT)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 180 Seats (Official portal verification via /btech/bt; historical legacy print materials listed 60 seats)
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Programme Overview
Biotechnology integrates biological sciences with engineering principles to develop solutions for healthcare, agriculture, pharmaceutical formulation, and environmental management.

## 3. Curricular Modules & Labs
- Molecular Biology and Genetic Engineering
- Microbiology and Fermentation Technology
- Bioprocess Engineering & Downstream Processing
- Immunology and Diagnostic Technologies
- Bioinformatics & Computational Biology

## 4. Career Opportunities
Graduates join biopharmaceutical companies, research laboratories, clinical trial organizations, and agricultural biotechnology firms.
