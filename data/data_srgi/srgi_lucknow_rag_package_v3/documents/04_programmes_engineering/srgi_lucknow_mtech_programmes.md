# Master of Technology (M.Tech) Postgraduate Programmes

## Scope
Postgraduate engineering specializations in Computer Science & Engineering and Mechanical Engineering (Production), 2-year duration, intake of 18 seats each.

## 1. Programme Overview
- **Degree Awarded:** Master of Technology (M.Tech)
- **Programme Duration:** 2 Academic Years (4 Semesters)
- **Regulatory Approval:** AICTE, New Delhi
- **University Affiliation:** Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
- **Target Audience:** Engineering graduates and working professionals aspiring for advanced research, R&D corporate roles, tech leadership, or doctoral academia.

## 2. Postgraduate Disciplines & Sanctioned Intake

| M.Tech Specialization | Sanctioned Annual Intake | Curricular Core |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)** | 18 Seats | Advanced Algorithms, Machine Learning Architectures, Cloud Infrastructure, High-Performance Computing, Distributed Systems. |
| **Mechanical Engineering (Production)** | 18 Seats | Advanced Manufacturing Processes, Automated Production Systems, Industrial Robotics, Supply Chain Engineering, CAD/CAM Optimization. |

## 3. Eligibility & Admission
- Candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree in relevant engineering branch (B.E. / B.Tech) with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for reserved category).
- Preference is accorded to GATE-qualified candidates, followed by university admission counselling merit.
