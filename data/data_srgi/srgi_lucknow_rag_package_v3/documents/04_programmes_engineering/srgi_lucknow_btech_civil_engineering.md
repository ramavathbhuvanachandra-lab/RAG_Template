# B.Tech in Civil Engineering (CE)

## Scope
Civil Engineering overview, structural engineering, environmental infrastructure, surveying, transportation engineering, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering (CE)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Departmental Scope
The Civil Engineering programme trains students to plan, design, construct, and maintain public and private infrastructure, including highways, bridges, urban transit, water resources, and residential complexes.

## 3. Specialized Laboratories
- Structural Analysis & Concrete Technology Lab
- Geotechnical & Soil Mechanics Lab
- Surveying Laboratory (Total Station, Theodolites, Digital Levels)
- Environmental Engineering & Water Quality Testing Lab
- Transportation Engineering Lab

## 4. Professional Pathways
Structural Engineers, Site Engineers, Geotechnical Analysts, and Project Management consultants.
