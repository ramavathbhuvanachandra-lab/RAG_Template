# B.Tech in Information Technology (IT)

## Scope
Information Technology degree details, web services, enterprise information systems, cybersecurity, sanctioned intake of 60 seats, and industry demand.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology (IT)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Programme Highlights
Information Technology focuses on the practical application, deployment, configuration, and security of enterprise computing resources, networked applications, and business software suites.

## 3. Key Learning Areas
- Cloud Computing Architecture & Virtualization
- Enterprise Database Design & SQL/NoSQL Systems
- Web Applications Development & API Architecture
- Cyber Laws, Network Defense & Ethical Hacking
- Mobile Computing and Wireless Sensor Networks

## 4. Industry Outcomes
Graduates work as IT Consultants, DevOps Engineers, Cloud Administrators, and Enterprise Systems Engineers across multinational IT services firms.
