# B.Tech in Agricultural Engineering (AG)

## Scope
Agricultural Engineering degree details, farm mechanization, soil and water conservation, food process engineering, sanctioned intake of 60 seats.

## 1. Programme Specification
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Technology in Agricultural Engineering (AG)
- **Sanctioned Annual Intake:** 60 Seats
- **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Affiliation:** AKTU, Lucknow

## 2. Departmental Scope
Agricultural Engineering applies engineering principles to agricultural production, farm power machinery, soil and water conservation, precision farming, and post-harvest food processing.

## 3. Laboratory & Field Resources
- Farm Power and Tractors Laboratory
- Soil & Water Conservation Engineering Lab
- Irrigation and Drainage Engineering Field Stations
- Post-Harvest Technology and Food Processing Units
- Agricultural Structures and Environmental Control

## 4. Professional Outcomes
Graduates work with farm machinery manufacturers, irrigation equipment corporations, agro-processing industries, and state agricultural departments.
