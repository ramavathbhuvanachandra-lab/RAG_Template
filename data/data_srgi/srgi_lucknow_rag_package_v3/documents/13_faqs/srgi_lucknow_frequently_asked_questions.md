# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — Admissions, Academics and Life at SRGI

## Scope
Comprehensive answers to the most common queries regarding college founding, approvals, engineering branches, fees, hostels, placements, and campus rules.

## 1. General & Institutional History
**Q1: When was S. R. Institute of Management and Technology established?**  
**A:** SRIMT was established in 2009 by Shri Pawan Singh Chauhan under the Subedar Raj Devi Educational Trust.

**Q2: In whose memory is the institution founded?**  
**A:** The institution is founded in loving memory of Late Shri Subedar Singh and Late Smt. Raj Devi Singh.

**Q3: What is the motto and vision of SRGI Lucknow?**  
**A:** The motto is *"Education Leads to Enlightenment"*, driven by the tri-fold vision of *"Shiksha, Sanskar aur Rozgar"*.

**Q4: Where is the campus located?**  
**A:** The campus is situated on NH-24, Sitapur Road, Bakshi ka Talab (BKT), Lucknow - 226201, Uttar Pradesh.

## 2. Academics & Programmes
**Q5: Which engineering disciplines are offered at B.Tech level?**  
**A:** B.Tech is offered in 9 disciplines: CSE (AI & ML, AI & DS), CS, IT, ECE, EEE/EN, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Biotechnology, and Agricultural Engineering.

**Q6: What is the annual intake for B.Tech CSE?**  
**A:** The core B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering programme has a sanctioned annual intake of 180 seats (verified via official portal /btech/cs). In addition, specialized emerging-technology tracks are offered with dedicated intakes: CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) with 60 seats, CSE (Artificial Intelligence) with 60 seats, and CSE (Data Science) with 60 seats. (Note: An older prospectus/FAQ version noted 120 seats for core CSE prior to seat expansion).

**Q7: Does SRGI offer post-graduate engineering degrees?**  
**A:** Yes, M.Tech is offered in Computer Science & Engineering (18 seats) and Mechanical Engineering Production (18 seats).

**Q8: Are management degrees available right after Class 12?**  
**A:** Yes, SRGI offers a 5-Year Integrated MBA (IMBA) for 10+2 school leavers, alongside the standard 2-Year MBA (240 seats) and 2-Year AGMP (60 seats).

**Q9: What medical and healthcare courses are offered?**  
**A:** D.Pharma (50 seats), Nursing (100 seats), and Diploma in Homeopathic Pharmacy (100 seats).

## 3. Placements & Campus Life
**Q10: What is the highest package achieved at SRGI placements?**  
**A:** The highest package recorded is 42.00 LPA, with an average package of 4.00 LPA and an overall 95.6% placement rate among eligible students. A cumulative total of over 24,888+ job opportunities have been generated by 900+ corporate recruiters.

**Q11: Are hostel facilities available for outstation students?**  
**A:** Yes, separate boys' and girls' hostels accommodate over 2,000+ students with 24-hour security, gym, Wi-Fi, and mess dining.

**Q12: How can I reach the admission office?**  
**A:** Contact the official admission team at +91 9793000005/6/7 or via email at info@srgi.in.
