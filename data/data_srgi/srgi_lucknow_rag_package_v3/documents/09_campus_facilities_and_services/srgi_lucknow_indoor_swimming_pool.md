# Indoor Olympic-Standard Swimming Pool (6-Track)

## Scope
National standard 6-track Olympic-style indoor swimming pool, certified lifeguards, advanced filtration and chlorination, coaching schedules.

## 1. Aquatic Facility Overview
S. R. Group of Institutions features an indoor **6-track national standard Olympic-style swimming pool** serving both competitive aquatic athletes and recreational swimmers.

## 2. Infrastructure & Hygiene Specifications
- **6-Track Layout:** Engineered for competitive freestyle, backstroke, and water polo tournaments.
- **Continuous Water Treatment:** Advanced continuous sand filtration and automated chlorination systems ensuring pristine water chemistry.
- **Safety First:** Certified professional lifeguards and coaches stationed on deck during all operational hours.
- **Separate Timings:** Dedicated morning and evening swimming slots for male and female students and faculty.
- **Ancillary Amenities:** Modern changing rooms, heated shower stalls, and secure lockers.
