# Transport Fleet and Bus Connectivity Across Lucknow

## Scope
Extensive institutional bus transit network connecting major Lucknow city nodes and surrounding suburbs, GPS tracking, security protocols, route coordinators.

## 1. Transit Fleet Overview
SRGI operates a comprehensive, modern fleet of institutional buses catering to day scholars and faculty commuting from diverse sectors across Lucknow city and surrounding regions.

## 2. Operational Highlights & Safety Protocols
- **Extensive Geographic Reach:** Covering Charbagh, Alambagh, Gomti Nagar, Indiranagar, Hazratganj, Chowk, Dubagga, Jankipuram, Sitapur Road, and adjoining townships.
- **Real-Time GPS Tracking:** All buses are integrated with GPS tracking units monitored at the central transit control room for student punctuality and route adherence.
- **Security & Staffing:** Licensed, professionally trained drivers, accompanied by dedicated route staff coordinators and emergency helpline access (+91 9793000005).
- **Subsidized Fare Structure:** Budget-friendly transport fees structured to ease financial pressure on guardians, especially during the first year of enrollment.
