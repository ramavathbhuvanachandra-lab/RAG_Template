# 65-Acre Sports Complex, Cricket Ground and Gymnasium

## Scope
Expansive 65-acre campus sporting complex, professional cricket arena, Hostels Sports League, annual Shaurya meetup, athletics, courts, and gym.

## 1. 65-Acre Sports Infrastructure
SRGI Lucknow takes pride in its expansive **65-acre overall campus sports complex**, reflecting the institution's commitment to physical vigor and competitive athletic spirit.

## 2. Key Sporting Arenas
- **Professional Cricket Ground:** Dedicated turf wicket and grass outfield utilized for inter-collegiate tournaments and the annual *Hostel Sports League*.
- **Athletic Tracks:** 400-meter running track for track-and-field events.
- **Court Sports:** Dedicated outdoor basketball, volleyball, and badminton courts with evening floodlights.
- **Modern Indoor Gymnasium:** High-grade strength training, cardio treadmills, cross-trainers, and dedicated fitness trainers.
- **Annual Flagship Meet — Shaurya:** The annual sports festival *Shaurya* unites hundreds of student athletes competing across 15+ disciplines.
