# Hostel Accommodation and Residential Life (2,000+ Capacity)

## Scope
Details of student hostels, separate boys and girls wings, 2000+ capacity, room furnishings, 24-hour security, gym, Wi-Fi, dining, and amphitheatre.

## 1. Residential Overview
The SRGI Lucknow residential complex provides a secure, disciplined, and comfortable home-like environment for over **2,000+ resident students**. Separate, self-contained residential wings are maintained for male and female scholars with round-the-clock administrative oversight.

## 2. Infrastructure & In-Room Amenities
- **Furnishings:** Every resident receives an individual bed, wardrobe, study table, and ergonomic chair.
- **Ventilation:** Spacious, naturally ventilated, and sunlit rooms with options for double and triple occupancy.
- **Round-the-Clock Security:** 24-hour perimeter security, CCTV monitoring, and strict biometric/logbook gate control supervised by the Proctorial Board.
- **Connectivity:** High-speed optical fiber Wi-Fi access throughout all hostel blocks.
- **Power & Utilities:** Uninterrupted power backup through dedicated high-capacity diesel generators; continuous purified RO water supply with hot water geysers in winter.

## 3. Lifestyle, Recreation & Health
- **Separate Gymnasiums:** Dedicated fitness gyms for boys and girls equipped with modern cardio and weight machines.
- **Recreation Rooms:** Common television rooms, indoor table tennis, and carrom facilities in each wing.
- **350+ Capacity Open-Air Amphitheatre:** Central amphitheatre for cultural gatherings, film screenings, and hostel celebrations.
- **Jogging Track:** Landscaped outdoor jogging trails spanning the hostel perimeter.
- **Banking & ATM:** On-campus banking facility and ATM access for resident convenience.
