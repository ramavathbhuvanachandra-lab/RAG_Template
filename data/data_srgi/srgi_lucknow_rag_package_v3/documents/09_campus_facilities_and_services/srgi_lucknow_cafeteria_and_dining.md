# Cafeteria and Campus Dining Services

## Scope
Campus cafeteria infrastructure, hygienic food preparation, nutritious multi-cuisine menus, dining capacity, student relaxation spaces.

## 1. Dining Philosophy
The SRGI Cafeteria serves as a vibrant social and culinary center on campus, offering hygienic, nutritious, and balanced meals, refreshments, and snacks throughout the academic day.

## 2. Operational Standards
- **Quality & Hygiene:** Regular inspections conducted by the institutional health committee; fresh ingredients sourced daily.
- **Menu Diversity:** Wholesome North and South Indian vegetarian meals, continental snacks, fresh juices, and hot beverages.
- **Seating & Ambience:** Expansive, well-ventilated indoor and semi-open seating accommodating hundreds of diners simultaneously.
