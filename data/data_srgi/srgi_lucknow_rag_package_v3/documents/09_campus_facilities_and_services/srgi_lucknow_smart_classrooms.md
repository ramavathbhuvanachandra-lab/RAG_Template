# Smart Classrooms and Digital Learning Spaces

## Scope
Audio-visual smart classrooms, interactive digital touch panels, acoustic architecture, hybrid lecture capabilities across academic blocks.

## 1. Modern Pedagogical Spaces
All major lecture theatres and classrooms across SRGI are upgraded into **Smart Classrooms** equipped with high-definition digital projectors, interactive touchscreens, and acoustic amplification systems.

## 2. Technology Capabilities
- Multi-media lecture delivery integrating slides, 3D simulations, and video demonstrations.
- Hybrid connectivity supporting live remote webinars and guest talks.
- Ergonomic, tiered seating configurations ensuring unobstructed visibility.
