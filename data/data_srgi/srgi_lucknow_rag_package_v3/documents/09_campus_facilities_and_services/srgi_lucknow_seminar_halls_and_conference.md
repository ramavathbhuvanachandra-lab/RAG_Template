# Conference Rooms and Seminar Halls

## Scope
Central conference room, executive boardrooms, multi-seat air-conditioned seminar halls for academic symposia, corporate meetings, and guest lectures.

## 1. Event Venues
The institution houses multiple centrally air-conditioned seminar halls and executive conference rooms designed for symposia, national conferences, academic defense presentations, and corporate recruiting presentations.

## 2. Facilities
- Integrated Dolby/JBL digital sound systems and multi-angle presentation screens.
- Executive conference roundtables for Board of Directors and Academic Council sessions.
- Capacity ranging from 150 to 500 attendees across individual halls.
