# Futuristic Laboratories and Technical Infrastructure

## Scope
High-tech engineering, computing, electronics, mechanical, and pharmaceutical laboratories, industrial-grade equipment, practical experimentation.

## 1. Laboratory Mandate
SRGI Lucknow maintains over 40 specialized departmental laboratories and mechanical workshops engineered to translate textbook theory into tangible technical mastery.

## 2. Key Laboratory Clusters
- **Advanced Computing Labs:** High-speed Intel multi-core workstations configured with dual OS (Ubuntu Linux / Windows), specialized AI/ML environments, Oracle/MySQL databases, and MATLAB suites.
- **Electronics & IoT Labs:** Digital storage oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, FPGA development boards, and IoT sensor arrays.
- **Mechanical Machine Shop & CAD/CAM:** Precision lathes, milling machines, universal testing machines (UTM), and licensed SolidWorks/AutoCAD workstations.
- **Civil & Geotechnical Testing:** Compression testing machines, surveying total stations, triaxial soil testing apparatus.
- **Pharmaceutical & Biotech Laboratories:** Sterile laminar airflow benches, spectrophotometers, dissolution apparatus, and incubation chambers.
