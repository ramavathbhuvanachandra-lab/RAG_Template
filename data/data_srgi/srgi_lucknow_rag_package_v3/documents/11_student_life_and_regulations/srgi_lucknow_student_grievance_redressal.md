# Student Grievance Redressal Mechanism and Support Portal

## Scope
This document outlines the student grievance redressal mechanism, the online submission portal, mandatory form fields, grievance categories, and administrative escalation pathways at S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow, derived directly from the official web portal.

## Official Grievance Redressal Portal
SRGI Lucknow maintains an online Student Grievance Portal accessible directly on the official website:
- **Portal URL:** [https://srgi.in/student-grievances](https://srgi.in/student-grievances)
- **Official Policy Statement:** *"We are here to listen. Please fill out the form below, and your concern will be securely recorded and addressed by the administration."*

## Online Submission Form Specifications
Students submitting a grievance through the official portal are required to provide the following structured data:

| Field Name | Input Type | Description / Accepted Options |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Name** | Text Field | Complete official student name as enrolled. |
| **Roll / Scholar Number** | Text Field | Official university roll number or institute scholar ID. |
| **Department / Course** | Dropdown Selector | B.Tech, M.Tech, B.Voc, MBA, IMBA, Medical Sciences, Other. |
| **Nature of Grievance** | Dropdown Selector | Categorized concern (see classification below). |
| **Detailed Description** | Multi-line Text Area | Comprehensive narrative explanation of the grievance. |
| **Submission Action** | Form Button | "Submit Grievance" initiates administrative recording. |

## Official Grievance Categories

| Category | Typical Scope & Concerns Handled |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Academic** | Sessional examination routines, classroom instruction, laboratory equipment access, practical evaluations, attendance discrepancies. |
| **Infrastructure** | Hostel room facilities, canteen & mess food hygiene, Wi-Fi connectivity, electrical / plumbing maintenance. |
| **Administrative** | Fee payment verification, receipts, scholarship document processing, mark-sheet issuance, transfer certificates. |
| **Harassment / Bullying** | Interpersonal peer issues, disrespectful behavior, unfair treatment, safety concerns. |
| **Other** | Transit bus logistics, library loan issues, general campus inquiries. |
