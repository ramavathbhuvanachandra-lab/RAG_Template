# Anti-Ragging Committee, Policies and Zero-Tolerance Mandate

## Scope
This document sets forth the statutory zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy, regulatory framework, and proctorial disciplinary channels governing all academic, sports, and residential facilities of S. R. Group of Institutions (SRGI), Lucknow.

## Statutory Zero-Tolerance Mandate
Ragging in any form — including verbal abuse, physical harassment, psychological intimidation, teasing, or forced conduct — is strictly prohibited across all campuses, classrooms, laboratories, buses, and hostels of S. R. Group of Institutions.

The institution strictly adheres to:
- Directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India
- University Grants Commission (UGC) Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions
- All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) Anti-Ragging Guidelines
- Uttar Pradesh Prohibition of Ragging in Educational Institutions Act
